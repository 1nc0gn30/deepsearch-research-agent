3. Modality Comparison Matrix

Superconducting Circuits

Pros: Nanosecond gate speeds (20-40 ns), mature lithography fabrication, ultrafast FPGA active decoding loops.

Cons: Cryogenic dilution wiring bottlenecks, 2D planar planar nearest-neighbor routing limits.

Neutral Atom Arrays

Pros: Dynamic optical tweezer shuttling, all-to-all logical connectivity, massive scaling (256+ atoms), qLDPC suitability.

Cons: Millisecond gate durations, optical trap scattering loss, laser power distribution scaling.

Trapped Ion QCCD

Pros: Highest single- and two-qubit gate fidelities (99.91%), near-zero cross-talk, long $T_1$ coherence (>10s).

Cons: Slower shuttling speed across multi-zone traps, micro-fabricated RF trap heating.
