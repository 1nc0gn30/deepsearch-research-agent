The 5-Stage Synthesis Pipeline

  2. Adaptive Multi-Engine Harvesting: Queries scientific indexes, regulatory filings, and primary literature using precise Boolean filters.
  3. Domain Authority & Credibility Scoring: Computes document credibility coefficients $\alpha_{cred} \in [0, 100]$ based on institutional trust, peer review, and recency decay.
  4. Dialectical Consensus & Conflict Resolution: Partitions claims into corroborating vs contradicting clusters, isolating minority viewpoints and unverified assumptions.
  5. Grounded Multi-Perspective Synthesis: Constructs formal citation graphs with zero unreferenced claims and calibrated confidence metrics.
